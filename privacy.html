Lantern Stories Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Lantern Stories is designed to help parents and caregivers read gentle, personalized stories with children.

Information You Enter

The app allows users to enter a child's first name and choose story preferences. Based on the current app design, these details are used to personalize stories within the app and are not intentionally sent to us or stored on our servers.

Data Collection

Lantern Stories does not intentionally collect personal information, create user accounts, or use advertising or analytics services. We do not sell personal information.

External Links

The app may contain links to educational resources on third-party websites. If you open these links, those websites may process information under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Lantern Stories is intended for shared use by parents, caregivers, and children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email eddevinesr@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when our app or privacy practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
