Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Lantern Stories for?
Parents and caregivers who enjoy reading supportive stories with children.

Can I personalize a story?
Lantern Stories offers story options based on a child's age and selected preferences.

Is Lantern Stories a substitute for professional advice?
No. Our stories are intended for shared reading and conversation, not medical or mental health diagnosis or treatment.
